Decisions

Settled and still open

Settled

  • Functional flier, single-wing bench rig first (D-01).
  • Four independent brushless drives, one per wing (D-03, D-05).
  • Crank-rocker: 90° stroke, equal up and down strokes, transmission angle at least 43.8° (D-04).
  • 25:1 in two stages, gear set P1: brass, POM, hardened steel, 7075 aluminium; steel crank gear P3 as fallback (D-05).
  • Pin-and-spring pitch joint, ±45° stops (D-06).
  • Wing 190 × 80 mm, membrane from r 50 mm, stepped carbon tube spar (D-12).
  • Stroke offset: servos on w1, w2 at 15:1 (±6°, limit ±6.5°), screw locks on w3, w4 (D-07).
  • Yaw by split-cycle, envelope requirement, firmware ε cap 0.06 (D-13).
  • AS5048A crank encoders at 1 kHz (D-08); Crazyflie Bolt 1.1 (D-09).
  • Stage 1 layout: 110 mm fore-hind, 20 mm hinge offset, 420 mm span, battery slide, tripod skids, swappable modules.
  • CAD and MuJoCo frame conventions fixed.

Open

  • Motor winding resistance and heat. Decides 2S, 3S (option X) or a new motor. First bench measurement.
  • Lift direction. If lift runs along the hinge axis (model A), DFLY would hover 59° nose-up. The rig must measure force direction in two axes.
  • Mass margin 0.1 g against the 5% target. The 10.3 g frame assumes pocketed plates not yet designed.
  • Flight gears are custom with no supplier quoted. Stage 1 contact safety factor 0.99, bench-gated. Ordered after the bench torque test.
  • ESC model not chosen.
  • Skids tip over at 19°.
  • Phase C has 5.6 g left; needs re-scoping.
  • Motor bell diameter and 4.3 mm shaft protrusion to measure.
  • Firmware: second servo channel at 1 µs, split-cycle controller, ε cap, yaw priority.

What happens next

  1. Stage 2: detail the wing-drive module (one wing plus its drive, which is also the bench rig). Claude Code generates STEP reference models and part drawings from design.yaml; the SOLIDWORKS model is the master, checked against them.
  2. Design the bench rig frame with a two-axis load cell, plus encoder, current-sensor and thermocouple mounts.
  3. Order the bench parts in one shipment: motors, packs, servo, encoders and magnets, bearings, pins, stock steel gears, thermocouple reader, current sensor.
  4. First measurements: winding resistance, heat, pack resistance, current limit. Pick 2S, 3S or a new motor.
  5. Single-wing tests: lift and its direction, power, crank torque, split-cycle yaw force, 20 h wear. Then resize and order the flight gears.
